# Clinic workflows

This describes the intended scope of a personal application in development. It does not assert that every workflow is finished or deployed.

| Role | Intended responsibility | Product concern |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Receptionist | Appointments and daily visit administration | A clear schedule and appropriate administrative visibility |
| Doctor | Patient records and visits | Relevant clinical context and continuity |
| Administrator | Clinic setup and staff responsibilities | Configuration and appropriate access boundaries |

Appointments, records, and administration belong to one clinic workflow, but staff interact with different parts of it. The product direction connects these activities while keeping responsibilities explicit.

The interface is intended for English, Arabic, and French. Arabic requires right-to-left layouts as well as translated text. Local operation and later synchronization account for connectivity interruptions.

## Public showcase boundary

There are no real patients, clinical examples, or appointment records here. Source stays private. Production use, privacy controls, recovery, and synchronization correctness require implementation-specific evidence beyond this overview.
